4. Artificial Intelligence, Kinematics & Traffic Optimization Models

🚗 Intelligent Driver Model (IDM) Physics

Vehicles follow continuous non-linear differential equations governing acceleration:

a = a_max * [ 1 - (v/v0)^4 - (s*(v, Δv) / s)^2 ]

Where s* represents the dynamic desired headway, factoring safe stopping distance, driver reaction time (1.0s), and comfortable deceleration (2.0 m/s²).

⏱️ Webster Demand-Responsive Optimization

Dynamically calculates allocated green times based on queue accumulation and saturation headways:

Green = clamp(G_min, t_lost + (Queue/lanes * h_sat), G_max)

Features automatic Gap-Out Early Clear: if an approach clears before maximum green expires, the controller transitions to yellow early to serve waiting cross-streets.

🤖 Deep Q-Network (RL DQN Controller)

Reinforcement learning policy evaluating state-action pairs Q(s, a). The reward function optimizes throughput while enforcing starvation equity bonuses (+1.5/sec) for neglected approaches.

🚦 Arterial Platoon Wave Progression

Synchronizes 3 consecutive junctions (Upstream Feeder, Central Main, Downstream Exit) along the metropolitan corridor at 50 km/h progression speeds, eliminating stop-and-go waves.
