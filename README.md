## 🌟 Executive Summary
**TrafficFlow-AI** is a production-grade, full-stack cyber-physical digital twin designed for modern Indian smart cities (benchmarked on a high-density **Metropolitan Arterial Corridor**). It pairs deep physical simulation (Intelligent Driver Model car-following kinematics, Poisson stochastic arrivals, dilemma zone safety) with sub-second AI signal optimization and Edge IoT civic infrastructure sensing.

The project directly solves critical urban civic and transportation challenges:
1. **Dynamic AI Signal Control:** Eliminates fixed-time gridlock via **Webster Demand-Responsive Saturation Headway** and **Deep Q-Network (RL DQN)** adaptive timing with starvation protection and early green gap-out.
2. **Edge IoT Smart Pole & Civic Health Detection:** Integrates edge perception for road surface potholes, municipal garbage overflows, urban waterlogging, and street SOS panic call points matching real-world lab prototypes.
3. **Emergency Vehicle Clearance:** Eliminates ambulance delays through directional corner pole acoustic sirens and dynamic green carpet preemption.
4. **Accident Detection & Bandwidth-Conscious Triage:** Detects collisions via simulated CCTV optical flow, G-force accelerometers, and acoustic signatures. Automatically dispatches 108 CAD emergency calls for critical crashes while **strictly suppressing calls for minor bumper scrapes** to protect emergency hospital bandwidth.
5. **Pedestrian & Vulnerable Road User (VRU) Safety:** Protects campus students and pedestrian crowd surges with an on-demand **Barnes Dance All-Pedestrian Scramble Phase**.
6. **Multi-Intersection Arterial Coordination:** Synchronizes consecutive junctions along the metropolitan arterial corridor in a cooperative green wave.
7. **Indian Smart City Economics & ESG Analytics:** Quantifies savings in **₹ INR**, **₹ in Lakhs/Year**, fuel liters, and commuter delay value according to Indian Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) standards.

### 1. 🕳️ Road Surface Pothole & Fissure Detection (PWD Work Orders)
* **Computer Vision Model:** Road surface segmentation identifies crater diameter, depth severity index, and edge cracking.
* **Autonomous Action:** When a critical crater ($\ge 25\text{ cm}$) is detected, an automated **Public Works Department (PWD)** road repair ticket is dispatched with exact GPS coordinates.
* **3D Visual Indicator:** Rendered in the 3D digital twin on Road A and Road C with broken asphalt textures and pulsating amber inspection halos.

### 2. 🗑️ Municipal Waste Overflow & Litter Clutter (Solid Waste SLA)
* **Computer Vision Model:** Monitored municipal bins track fill volume, lid obstruction, and perimeter litter scatter (cardboard boxes, paper cups, plastic bottles).
* **Autonomous Action:** Triggers automated **Solid Waste Management (SWM)** route reassignment when bin capacity exceeds 85% or ground litter radius exceeds 2.5 meters.
* **3D Visual Indicator:** Modelled in the 3D scene with green municipal bin bodies, yellow access lids, and surrounding scattered litter debris.

### 3. 🌊 Urban Curb Waterlogging & Drainage Blockage
* **Computer Vision Model:** Hydrological curb monitoring detects standing water depth and drain gutter flow impedance.
* **Autonomous Action:** Warns motorists of hydroplaning risk and generates municipal stormwater drain maintenance alerts.
* **3D Visual Indicator:** Specular-reflective water puddle with animated concentric ripple effects along the pedestrian curb gutter.

### 4. 🗼 Physical Smart Pole Edge IoT Prototype
* **Lab Hardware Form Factor:** Faithfully modeled to replicate the physical lab prototype:
  * Square top solar energy harvesting canopy.
  * Flanged cylindrical grey mast anchored with corner mounting bolts.
  * Downward luminaire elbow bracket with self-luminous LED street bulb.

  * Upper sensor slot housing environmental sensors (MQ-135 Air Quality & mmWave Radar).
  * Lower interactive slot featuring OLED notification display and 112 SOS Panic Emergency Button.

## ⚡ Core Engineering Capabilities

### 1. Directional Corner Pole Sirens & Green Carpet Wave
* **Hardware Conceptualization:** 4 high-mast corner poles positioned at the intersection (Pole A - North, Pole B - East, Pole C - South, Pole D - West), equipped with 115 dB directional acoustic horns and high-intensity strobe LED beacons.
* **Intelligent Operation:** When an oncoming 108 Ambulance or Fire Tender is detected on a specific approach road, **only the siren pole facing that road activates**.
* **Visual & Acoustic Feedback:** The 3D viewport renders rotating beacon strobes and directional acoustic indicators. Conflicting traffic approaches smoothly hold at red while the emergency vehicle enjoys an unobstructed green carpet corridor.

### 2. AI Accident Detection & Sub-Second Severity Triage
* **Multi-Sensor Fusion:** Combines CCTV optical flow vector anomalies, roadside acoustic shockwave microphones, and vehicle telemetry G-force decelerations.
* **Sub-Second AI Triage Decision:**
  * **Critical Collision ($\ge 45\text{ km/h}$, $6.8\text{G}$ deceleration, airbags deployed, cabin intrusion):**
    * System Verdict: **CRITICAL EMERGENCY**.
    * Auto-CAD Action: Immediately dials **108 Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD)** to the **City Multi-Specialty Trauma Care & Emergency Center**.
    * Telemetry Transmitted: Exact GPS coordinates (`11.0245° N, 76.9982° E`), vehicle count, injury estimate, and dispatch code `108-DISPATCH-METRO`.
    * Traffic Response: **Perimeter Lockdown** holding upstream roads at Red while granting downstream clearance to prevent secondary pileups.
  * **Minor Fender-Bender ($14\text{ km/h}$, $1.3\text{G}$ deceleration, airbags intact, 0 casualties):**
    * System Verdict: **MINOR INCIDENT — 108 CALL SUPPRESSED**.
    * Rationale: **Preserves emergency hospital lines and ambulance availability for genuine life-threatening emergencies.**
    * Local Resolution: Generates digital insurance incident packet and initiates an advisory lane flush to restore normal flow.

### 3. Pedestrian VRU Safety & Barnes Dance Scramble Phase
* **Context:** Modeled after student pedestrian surges near University & College Educational Campuses along the arterial corridor.
* **Operation:** Pedestrians press physical crossing pushbuttons on crosswalks.
* **Crowd Surge / Scramble Mode:** During heavy pedestrian volumes, the system initiates a **Barnes Dance All-Pedestrian Scramble Phase**.
  * All vehicular signals hold on **ALL-RED**.
  * All 4 crosswalk zebra stripes illuminate in radiant neon green.
  * Pedestrians cross both orthogonally and diagonally with complete safety from turning vehicles.

### 4. Indian Smart City Economic & ESG ROI Engine
* **Localized Financial Grounding:**
  * Petrol / Diesel savings calculated at **₹102.50 / Liter**.
  * Commuter delay value benchmarked at **₹150.00 / Commuter Hour** (MoRTH guidelines).
  * Direct reporting in **₹ INR Saved**, **₹ in Lakhs/Year Saved**, and Clean Air Index rating.
* **Comparative Dual-Twin:** Simultaneously runs Fixed-Time control alongside AI Adaptive control under identical traffic seeds, demonstrating 20–45% reductions in waiting times and fuel waste.

## 🚀 How to Run the Prototype

### Prerequisites
* Python 3.9+ (Tested on Python 3.10, 3.11, 3.12, 3.13, 3.14 on Windows, macOS, and Linux).
* Modern web browser with WebGL support (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
* Standard Python libraries only — **no external heavy C++ or SUMO dependencies required!**

### Launching the Application
Open a terminal in the project directory and run:

```bash
python run.py
```

Or run the server directly:
```bash
python server.py
```

### Accessing the Web Dashboard
Open your modern browser and navigate to:
```
http://localhost:8080
```

### Running Automated Integration Tests
To execute the automated 14-suite end-to-end verification test:
```bash
python backend/test_fullstack.py
```
